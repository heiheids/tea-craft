================================================================================
Part 1  Getting Started
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Obtaining a tea seed
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tea seeds cannot be crafted. The only way to get one is to kill zombies.

  A zombie may spawn holding a tea seed in its main hand.
  When killed, it ALWAYS drops the seed.

  Probability (identical to "zombie holding an iron shovel",
  derived by decompiling vanilla):

    Hard difficulty     5%  x 4/6  =  3.33%
    Other difficulties  1%  x 4/6  =  0.67%

  In practice: on Peaceful/Easy/Normal you need roughly 150 zombies per seed;
  on Hard, roughly 30.

  Note: holding the seed does not matter — we deliberately set it to
  "always drops on death" instead of the vanilla 8.5% equipment drop rate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2 Planting: temperature is everything
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Plant the tea seed on grass or dirt. It goes through 4 stages:

  age 0  Seedling       just planted
  age 1  Growing        random-tick growth (bonemeal works)
  age 2  Grown          full one block tall (bonemeal caps here)
  age 3  Harvestable    tender green leaves appear at the top
                        ONLY time advances past age 2 — bonemeal does nothing

  Important design note: bonemeal can speed up growth to a full bush,
  but it cannot produce harvestable leaves. You must wait for the timer.
  Those tender leaves at the top are the "ready" visual cue.

  Growth time (from planting to fully grown):
    Hottest biome   3 in-game days
    Coldest biome   8 in-game days
    Linear interpolation by temperature in between

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 Biome temperature -> tea quality (the most important table)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

At harvest time the leaves remember the biome they grew in.
That temperature decides the finished tea's QUALITY TIER,
and the tier decides the EFFECT DURATION.

  Biome temperature      Tier    Duration
  ---------------------  ------  -----------
  Tropical    >=  1.10   1       20 minutes
  Subtropical >=  0.85   2       40 minutes
  Temperate   >=  0.55   3       60 minutes
  Boreal      >=  0.15   4       80 minutes
  Polar       <   0.15   5      100 minutes

  The colder the biome, the better the tea.

  Origin information travels with the leaves the whole way:
  harvest -> every processing step -> finished tea -> brewed tea.
  It will never silently reset to "Temperate" during processing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.4 Boreal and Polar biomes: you need a campfire
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tea bushes in Boreal (tier 4) and Polar (tier 5) biomes need a CAMPFIRE
within a 2-block radius.

  No campfire  ->  the bush does not grow.
  Walk up to it and you will see: "It's too cold — put a campfire nearby."

  The campfire must be lit.

  This is the price of high-tier tea: the good stuff does not grow
  in comfortable places.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.5 Harvesting
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Right-click a fully grown bush (age 3).

  1 to 3 fresh leaves per harvest (random).
  The bush returns to age 2 and you can wait for the next flush.

  Fresh leaves come in 5 item variants by origin:
    Tropical / Subtropical / Temperate / Boreal / Polar leaves

  Fresh leaves cannot be eaten. The message "Finished tea can't be eaten
  directly — put it in a gaiwan and add water" also appears for fresh
  leaves, because they belong to the same item family.
  Their only use is going into a drying tray to start processing.


================================================================================
Part 2  Working Blocks
================================================================================

There are 8 processing blocks plus 5 furniture/teaware blocks (Part 4).

  Processing: tea bush / drying tray / rolling tray / tea wok /
              charcoal stove / clay kiln / potter's wheel / kettle
  Furniture:  tea table / hot tea table / tea chair / tea box / tea set

  Every processing block holds up to 32 tea items at once.
  The tea box is the exception (storage, 999 per slot).


================================================================================
2.1 Drying Tray
================================================================================

Purpose  Step one: dry fresh leaves into "withered leaves". Shared by all
         three production chains. Also handles shaking (Dancong only)
         and Dancong's kill-green step.

Recipe   3 wooden slabs + 3 planks (see appendix)

[Inserting]
  Right-click the tray while holding fresh leaves.
  It inserts the WHOLE STACK: whatever you are holding, up to the 32 cap.
  The tray only accepts one origin and one stage at a time
  (mixing is rejected).

[Drying]
  Wait 5 minutes (6000 ticks).
  Look at the tray to see a progress bar:
    "Drying Tray · Withering  45%"

  After 5 minutes it becomes "withered".

  Rain pauses drying.
    Condition: open sky + raining -> progress freezes (it does not reset).
    A block above the tray blocks the rain, so you can build a roof
    and keep drying.

[Removing]
  Right-click with an empty hand to take the tea out
  (it becomes a "withered leaves" item).

  Note: all three chains share this single "withered leaves" item.
  Which block you put it into next decides the route.

[Extra: shaking + kill-green for Phoenix Dancong]
  Dancong's steps 2 and 3 both happen on the drying tray.

  Step one: shaking
    Sneak + hold right-click for 3 seconds -> one shake.
    Three shakes total.

    While shaking the screen shows:
      "Drying Tray · Shaking"
      "After this shake: 2 / 3"
      "Progress 67%"

    After 3 shakes -> "shaken leaves" (dancong_3_shaking).

    Only withered Dancong leaves can be shaken. Shaking fresh leaves
    tells you to dry them first.

  Step two: kill-green
    Put the shaken leaves BACK on the drying tray and wait 5 minutes.
    They become "kill-green leaves" (dancong_4_killgreen).

    Note: Dancong's kill-green happens on the TRAY, not in the wok.
    This differs from green tea, where kill-green needs the wok.


================================================================================
2.2 Rolling Tray
================================================================================

Purpose  Rolling (green tea, black tea) and fermentation (all chains).
         Phoenix Dancong skips rolling — leaves go straight to fermentation.

Recipe   3 wooden slabs + 2 wooden fences

[Inserting]
  Right-click while holding the correct stage. Whole stack, up to 32.
  No mixing origins or stages.

[Rolling]
  Sneak + hold right-click for 3 seconds -> one roll.
  Three rolls total, 3 seconds each.

  The screen shows:
    "Rolling Tray · Rolling"
    "Roll 2 / 3  33%"

  After 3 rolls it moves to the fermentation stage.

  Dancong exception: Dancong leaves are NOT rolled. They start
  fermenting immediately, because traditional Dancong processing has
  no rolling step. The message reads "Rolling complete, leaving to ferment".

[Fermentation]
  Fermentation starts automatically after rolling and takes 30 seconds
  (600 ticks). During this the screen shows "Fermenting 62%".

  Do not try to roll during fermentation — you get
  "Fermenting, please wait".

[Removing]
  After fermentation completes, right-click with an empty hand.

    Green/Black -> "fermented leaves" or "rolled leaves"
                   (depending on the chain)
    Dancong     -> "fermented Dancong leaves"

  Green tea is special: green tea does NOT ferment.
  After 3 rolls you get "rolled leaves" and nothing else happens.
  That is intentional — green tea stops here and goes to the wok for drying.

[How to tell if fermentation finished]
  Right-click with an empty hand:
    "Fermenting, please wait"        -> not done yet
    "Already fermented, right-click to take" -> done, click again to take


================================================================================
2.3 Tea Wok
================================================================================

Purpose  Kill-green, drying, and first-bake. The second most-used block.

  Exactly three inputs are accepted (from the source):
    Withered leaves (green tea)  -> kill-green leaves   (green step 1)
    Rolled leaves (green tea)    -> dried leaves        (green step 2)
    Fermented leaves (black tea) -> first-bake leaves   (black step 1)

  Dancong does NOT go into the wok. Dancong's kill-green happens on
  the drying tray. Putting Dancong leaves in gives
  "The wok only accepts rolled tea".

Recipe   Iron block + wooden slab (see appendix)

[Inserting tea]
  Right-click the wok to open its GUI,
  or right-click while holding the tea (goes into the input slot).
  Up to 32 items. Accepts rolled tea only.

[Adding fuel]
  Stirring needs charcoal. Put charcoal in the fuel slot.
  Each stir consumes 1 charcoal.

[Stirring]
  Sneak + right-click -> stir once.
  Three stirs total.

  Messages:
    "Stirred 8 items"
    "Stirring... keep sneak-right-clicking (3 total)"
    "Stirring complete"

  Each stir processes 8 items. If you loaded 32, you need 4 rounds
  (3 stirs each). Large batches are click-heavy — batch it in smaller
  amounts if you value your wrist.

[Notes]
  "The wok only accepts rolled tea" — wrong stage is rejected.
  "No charcoal in the wok, can't stir" — add fuel first.


================================================================================
2.4 Charcoal Stove
================================================================================

Purpose  The final step: turn "last-stage" tea into finished tea.
         All three chains pass through here.

  Exactly three inputs are accepted (from the source):
    Dried leaves (green tea)     -> finished green tea
    First-bake leaves (black)    -> finished black tea
    Fermented leaves (Dancong)   -> finished Phoenix Dancong

  Only last-stage tea is accepted. Putting tea in too early is rejected.
  (The message "The stove only accepts fermented tea" was written for
   the black tea path but applies to all three.)

Recipe   3 wooden slabs + 2 wooden fences (similar to the rolling tray)

[Inserting tea]
  Right-click to open the GUI.
  Accepts only last-stage tea.

[Adding fuel]
  Put charcoal in the fuel slot. Each charcoal burns for 1600 ticks (80 s).

[Baking]
  Processes 8 items at a time and takes 30 seconds (600 ticks).
  Progress is visible in the GUI.

[Notes]
  "The stove only accepts fermented tea"
  "There is already other tea in the stove" — one kind at a time
  "Fuel is full (64 charcoal)"


================================================================================
2.5 Clay Kiln + Potter's Wheel
================================================================================

These two must be placed as a PAIR. The kiln sits directly next to the
potter's wheel, and blanks are sent from the wheel straight into the kiln.

  Placing them apart gives "Must be placed next to the potter's wheel".

[Using the potter's wheel]
  1. Right-click the wheel while holding clay balls -> into the blank slot
  2. Choose what to make in the GUI (pattern list):
       Tea cup       1 clay ball
       Gaiwan        2 clay balls
       Kettle        4 clay balls
  3. The finished blank is sent automatically into the adjacent kiln

[Using the kiln]
  1. Put coal or charcoal in the fuel slot
     One firing consumes 8 fuel items (coal or charcoal both count)
  2. One firing takes ONE IN-GAME DAY = 20 minutes of real time
     This really is 20 minutes, not a few seconds.
     Progress advances only with game ticks, so it does not finish
     while you are offline or the chunk is unloaded
     (same as the vanilla furnace).
     The kiln is designed for "load it and go do something else".
  3. Take the finished item from the output slot

  The kiln has 6 blank slots and 6 output slots, so it can fire 6 items
  in parallel.


================================================================================
2.6 Kettle
================================================================================

Purpose  Boil water and pour it to brew tea. Required for brewing.

Recipe   See appendix (must be fired in a kiln)

[Filling with water]
  Right-click the kettle while holding a water bucket or water bottle.
    Water bucket  -> consumes the bucket, returns an empty bucket
    Water bottle  -> consumes the bottle, returns a glass bottle

[Heating]
  Place the kettle on a HEAT SOURCE:
    - Magma block
    - Lit campfire
    - Lit furnace
    - Hot tea table (see Part 4)

  Boils in 5 seconds (100 ticks).
  White steam comes out of the spout when boiling.

  Look at it to check status:
    "Kettle · Empty"
    "Kettle · Boiling 45%"
    "Kettle · Boiled water"

[Brewing]
  Hold the kettle with boiled water and right-click a TEA TRAY
  that has dry tea in its gaiwan.
  -> dry tea becomes brewed tea.

  Each portion of dry tea yields 1 cup.
  So 3 portions -> 3 cups.

  Messages:
    "The kettle is empty — fill it and boil first"
    "Put tea leaves in the gaiwan before brewing"

NOTE: the TEAPOT is decorative and cannot boil water
  There is also a "Teapot" in the mod. It is purely decorative.
  Right-clicking it gives "This is a decorative teapot — use the
  'Kettle' if you want to boil water".
  The two models look similar; do not grab the wrong one.


================================================================================
Part 3  Tea Effects
================================================================================

Each of the three teas has a different effect.
All durations depend on QUALITY: 20/40/60/80/100 minutes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Green Tea -> Speed II + calming creepers and undead
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Effect: Speed II (+40% movement speed)
  Duration: 20/40/60/80/100 minutes by quality

  Extra effect (unique to green tea):
    Within a 32-block radius, CREEPERS and UNDEAD mobs that are
    targeting you LOSE THEIR TARGET and stop attacking you.

    Creepers: creepers
    Undead: zombies, skeletons, zombified piglins, wither skeletons,
            phantoms, etc.

    This does not make them vanish or friendly — they simply stop
    chasing you. If you hit them, they will fight back.

  Use case: caving, travelling, doing dangerous work.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 Black Tea -> Regeneration II + 90% less hunger drain
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Effect: Regeneration II (1 health every 2 seconds)
  Duration: 20/40/60/80/100 minutes

  Extra effect:
    HUNGER DRAIN REDUCED BY 90%
    Sprinting, mining and fighting consume only 1/10 of the normal
    saturation/exhaustion.

    This reduces drain; it does not refill hunger. Food lasts much
    longer, but an empty bar is still empty.

  Use case: long work sessions, AFK, long-distance travel.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 Phoenix Dancong -> Haste IX + no phantoms
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Effect: Haste IX
    amplifier 8 = Haste IX

    That is a very high level; mining speed increases substantially.
    (For reference: a fully powered beacon gives Haste II.)

  Duration: 20/40/60/80/100 minutes

  Extra effect:
    Phantoms neither attack you nor spawn.

    Implementation: the player's TIME_SINCE_REST statistic is
    continuously reset to zero. Vanilla uses exactly that stat to
    decide whether phantoms spawn on rainy nights. Zero means
    "just slept", so spawning never triggers.

    Phantoms already nearby (within 48 blocks) forget you as a target.

  Use case: players who never sleep, night activity, big mining projects.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 How to drink tea
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Finished tea CANNOT be eaten by right-clicking it.
  Doing so gives: "Finished tea can't be eaten directly — put it in a
  gaiwan and add water".

  The correct full flow:

    1. Place a tea tray on the ground (or on a tea table)
    2. Right-click the tray with a gaiwan   -> places the gaiwan
    3. Right-click the tray with tea cups    -> places cups (up to 3)
    4. Right-click the tray with finished tea -> dry tea into the gaiwan
                                                (up to 3 portions)
    5. Fill the kettle, put it on a heat source, boil
    6. Right-click the tray with the boiling kettle -> water in,
       dry tea becomes brewed tea
    7. Right-click the gaiwan on the tray to pour brewed tea into a cup
    8. Right-click the cup -> drink

  Every step gives feedback. If you get stuck, read the text near
  your crosshair.

  Can you skip the tray and drink straight from the gaiwan?
    No. Brewed tea must pass through the tray to reach the cups.
    To drink in the field, carry a gaiwan + a cup + a tea tray.


================================================================================
Part 4  Furniture and Teaware
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Tea Table
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A full block with a flat top. A tea tray sits flush on the surface,
  not floating.

  Recipe: 3 logs + 2 planks

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 Hot Tea Table
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A tea table with a magma block embedded in the centre. Put a kettle
  on it and it heats like it is sitting on magma — water boils
  automatically.

  It does NOT burn the player. Safe to stand on.

  Recipe: tea table + magma block (shapeless, irreversible)
  IRREVERSIBLE: breaking it drops "Hot Tea Table"; it does not
  convert back into a normal tea table.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 Tea Chair
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A sittable chair. Places in all four directions.

  Right-click with an empty hand -> sit
  Sneak + right-click            -> stand up

  The collision box only reaches seat height (about 0.59 blocks).
  The backrest has no collision, so you can walk through it from behind.

  While seated you can still interact with teaware on the tea table —
  no need to stand up.

  Recipe: 3 planks + 2 fences

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 Tea Box
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A wooden box for storing finished tea.

  Capacity: 27 slots, up to 999 of the same tea per slot

  Operations:
    Right-click while holding tea   -> store directly (no GUI needed)
    Right-click                     -> open the GUI
    Left-click a slot in the GUI    -> take 3
    Right-click a slot in the GUI   -> take 64
    Shift-click a slot in the GUI   -> take the whole slot
    Sneak + right-click             -> pick up the WHOLE BOX
                                       (tea comes with it)

  Only finished tea is accepted. Fresh leaves and intermediate
  products cannot go in.

  If your inventory is full you cannot pick up the box:
  "Inventory is full — cannot pick up the tea box".

  What if you just break the box?
    The box drops with all its tea inside. Pick it up, place it again,
    the tea is still there.
    (Creative mode drops nothing, same as vanilla.)

  Recipe: 8 wooden slabs in a ring

  Model: a flat wooden box 7 pixels tall with a 3x3 decorative lid.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.5 Tea Tray + Gaiwan + Tea Cups
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The most involved set, and the core of the tea experience.

[Tea Tray]
  A shallow wooden tray holding 1 gaiwan and up to 3 tea cups.

  Recipe: wooden slab + bamboo

[Placing the gaiwan]
  Right-click the tray while holding a gaiwan.

[Placing cups]
  Right-click the tray while holding a tea cup.
  Up to 3 cups, filling 3 positions in order.

[Adding dry tea]
  Right-click the tray while holding finished tea ->
  dry tea goes into the gaiwan (up to 3 portions).
  Each portion = 1 cup of brewed tea.

[Pouring water]
  Right-click the tray with a kettle of boiling water ->
  dry tea becomes brewed tea.

[Pouring into a cup]
  Right-click the gaiwan position on the tray ->
  brewed tea moves into an empty cup.

[Drinking]
  Right-click a cup containing brewed tea -> drink.
  -> gain the full effect of that tea (duration by brew quality).

[Taking teaware back]
  Right-click a piece of teaware with an empty hand -> take that piece.

[Taking the whole tray]
  Sneak + right-click -> take the entire tray
  (including gaiwan, cups and any brewed tea).

  Plain right-click on the tray rim (not on a piece of teaware)
  does NOTHING. This prevents accidental pickups.
  This was an explicit requirement: "if the tea tray is on a tea table,
  you should only be able to pick it up while sneaking".

  On a tea table the rule is the same: interacting with teaware works
  normally, but picking up the tray needs sneak.

[Tray status readout]
  Looking at the tray shows:
    Empty tea tray
    Teaware: gaiwan yes · cups 2
    Gaiwan dry tea: Phoenix Dancong · quality 4 · 2 portions
    Gaiwan brew: Phoenix Dancong · quality 4 · 2 cups left
    Cup 1: Phoenix Dancong · quality 4

  This lets you see the tray contents without opening any GUI.

================================================================================
Appendix B  Complete Processing Chains (verified against source)
================================================================================

All three chains share step 2, "withered leaves" (item id: spread_2_withered).
Which block you put it into decides the route:

    Into the TEA WOK         -> Green tea
    Into the ROLLING TRAY    -> Black tea
    Back onto the DRYING TRAY to shake -> Phoenix Dancong

This is the fork in the road for the entire mod.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Green Tea
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Fresh leaves
   |  drying tray (5 minutes)
  Withered leaves      spread_2_withered
   |  tea wok (3 stirs, 1 charcoal each)          <- kill-green
  Kill-green leaves    green_3_killgreen
   |  rolling tray (3 rolls, 3 seconds each)
  Rolled leaves        green_4_rolling
   |  tea wok (3 stirs)                           <- drying
  Dried leaves         green_5_drying
   |  charcoal stove (1 bake, 30 seconds, charcoal)
  Finished green tea   green_6_finished

  Effect: Speed II + creepers/undead stop attacking

  Note: green tea does NOT ferment. Rolled leaves never move on to
  fermentation. That is intentional.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Black Tea
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Fresh leaves
   |  drying tray (5 minutes)
  Withered leaves      spread_2_withered
   |  rolling tray (3 rolls)
  Rolled leaves        black_3_rolling
   |  rolling tray (wait 30 seconds to ferment, do not roll)
  Fermented leaves     black_4_fermenting
   |  tea wok (3 stirs)                           <- first bake
  First-bake leaves    black_5_firstbake
   |  charcoal stove (1 bake)
  Finished black tea   black_6_finished

  Effect: Regeneration II + 90% less hunger drain

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phoenix Dancong
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Fresh leaves
   |  drying tray (5 minutes)
  Withered leaves      spread_2_withered
   |  drying tray again (sneak + hold right-click 3 s, 3 shakes)  <- shaking
  Shaken leaves        dancong_3_shaking
   |  drying tray again (wait 5 more minutes)     <- kill-green (on the tray)
  Kill-green leaves    dancong_4_killgreen
   |  rolling tray (ferments immediately, no rolling, 30 s)
  Fermented leaves     dancong_6_roasting
   |  charcoal stove (1 bake)
  Finished Dancong     dancong_8_finished

  Effect: Haste IX + phantoms do not spawn and do not attack

  Notes:
    Dancong's kill-green happens on the DRYING TRAY, not in the wok.
    Dancong has no rolling step; kill-green leaves ferment directly.
    Dancong's final bake uses the CHARCOAL STOVE, not the wok.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Which block accepts what
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Block            Accepts              Produces              Note
  ---------------  -------------------  --------------------  -----------
  Drying tray      fresh leaves         withered leaves       5 minutes
  Drying tray      withered (Dancong)   shaken leaves         3 shakes
  Drying tray      shaken leaves        kill-green leaves     5 more min
  Rolling tray     withered (black)     rolled leaves         3 rolls
  Rolling tray     rolled (black)       fermented leaves      30 s wait
  Rolling tray     kill-green (Dancong) fermented leaves      no rolling
  Tea wok          withered (green)     kill-green leaves     3 stirs
  Tea wok          rolled (green)       dried leaves          3 stirs
  Tea wok          fermented (black)    first-bake leaves     3 stirs
  Charcoal stove   dried (green)        finished green tea    30 s bake
  Charcoal stove   first-bake (black)   finished black tea    30 s bake
  Charcoal stove   fermented (Dancong)  finished Dancong      30 s bake
  Clay kiln        clay blank           ceramic item          20 min/firing

  The wok does not accept Dancong. The charcoal stove only accepts
  last-stage tea. Wrong inputs are rejected with a specific message.


================================================================================
Appendix C  Troubleshooting
================================================================================

Q: My tea bush will not grow.
A: Check the biome. Boreal (tier 4) and Polar (tier 5) need a campfire
   within 2 blocks. Elsewhere it grows normally, just at different
   speeds (3 to 8 in-game days).

Q: Bonemeal does not produce harvestable leaves.
A: By design. Bonemeal caps at "grown" (age 2). Harvesting requires
   waiting for age 3. Tender green leaves appearing at the top means ready.

Q: All my tea is tier 3 — how do I get tier 5?
A: Grow it in a colder biome. Tier 5 needs a biome temperature below
   0.15 (Polar) — typically snowy mountains or ice plains.
   Bring a campfire.

Q: I cannot pick up the tea tray.
A: You must SNEAK + right-click. Plain right-click on empty space does
   nothing (anti-misclick). To take a single piece of teaware, just
   right-click that piece normally.

Q: I cannot get tea out of the tea box.
A: Open the GUI. Left-click takes 3, right-click takes 64,
   shift-click takes the whole slot.
   Sneak + right-click picks up the whole box — it does not pour tea out.

Q: The kettle does not boil on a tea table.
A: A normal tea table is not a heat source. Use a HOT TEA TABLE
   (tea table + magma block), or place the kettle directly on a magma
   block / lit campfire / lit furnace.

Q: Right-clicking finished tea does nothing.
A: Finished tea cannot be eaten directly. Go through the full brewing
   flow: tray -> gaiwan -> cups -> dry tea -> boiling water -> pour -> drink.

Q: Will I lose the tea inside if I break the tea box?
A: No. The box drops with all its tea inside.

Q: Can I mix different teas in one processing block?
A: No. A single block holds one origin and one stage at a time.
   Mixing gives "There is already tea of another stage on the tray".

Q: Does rain affect drying?
A: Yes. Open sky + rain pauses drying (it does not reset).
   A block above the tray blocks rain.
